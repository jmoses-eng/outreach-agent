## PR Confidence Scoring

- HIGH: covers home goods, local makers or gifts; has featured a product in the last 90 days; Port Ellery or river valley readership; actively publishing

## Wholesale Program

- Product line offered: Ferry Mug ($42 retail), Breakwater Bowl ($58 retail), Pier Vase ($34 retail)
- Wholesale price and margin: 50% of retail at any quantity. 55% margin for stores ordering 60+ pieces a quarter.
- Minimums, lead time, payment terms: opening order 12 pieces mixed, reorders 6 pieces. 2 to 3 weeks. First order prepaid, then net 30.
- What an account gets: product photography, a shelf card explaining the river glazes, replacement of anything that arrives damaged
- Wholesale sheet PDF: Tidewater_Wholesale_2026.pdf. Sent on reply or in follow-up 1, not in the first message.
- First-touch exception: a wholesale first message may state the retail price, the margin and the opening minimum, because store buyers decide on those. The full sheet still waits.

**Target store fit:** sells home goods or kitchenware in the $25 to $80 range, carries other independent makers, and its customers care where things are made.

**Target store types, in priority order:**
1. Independent home and kitchen shops
2. Museum and gallery shops
3. Specialty food and gourmet shops with a tableware section
4. Plant shops and florists (vases)
5. Boutique hotel shops

**The ask, by geography:**
- Port Ellery stores: "Could I bring a few pieces by this week?" Rosa visits in person. Never offer to ship samples to a Port Ellery store.
- Stores elsewhere: "Can I send you a mug and a bowl to see in person?"

**Hard disqualifiers (do not add, or disqualify on discovery):**
- The store makes or sells its own house ceramics. Check their site before adding.
- The store is already a stockist.
- Territory rule: no second stockist on the same street. Mill Row and Water Street each already have one.
- Never cold-pitch The Long Table (a co-op we are part of).

**Wholesale confidence scoring:**
- HIGH: home or kitchen focus, other independent makers on the shelves, $25 to $80 price range, active storefront
- MEDIUM: adjacent category (plants, food, stationery) with some tableware
- LOW: mass-market stock, price points under $20, or unclear curation

## Exclusive Partnership Program

**Model:** Tidewater becomes the only maker of a stoneware piece for one institution: a museum, a venue or a long-running event. The piece is made from material tied to that place and sold only there. We own the making; they own the story and the shelf. Our reference deal is the Conservancy's members-only river vase.

**Categories, seeds and required field research:**

1. **Museums and historic sites**
   - Seed targets: Port Ellery Maritime Museum, Halsey Mill Historic Site
   - Required research: the collection or history we would draw from; whether their shop already carries an exclusive maker line (if yes, disqualify); the shop buyer's name
   - Named contact required: yes. A shop inbox does not count.

2. **Restaurants with their own identity**
   - Seed targets: Oyster & Ember, The Fogline Kitchen
   - Required research: whether they already commission their tableware from a named potter (if yes, disqualify); the chef or owner who makes design decisions
   - Named contact required: yes

3. **Long-running festivals**
   - Seed targets: Ellery River Lantern Festival, Northgate Harvest Fair
   - Required research: who holds the official merchandise rights; whether a commemorative item already exists
   - Named contact required: yes, the merchandise or partnerships lead

**Concept rule:** before drafting, write a one-line concept rooted in what the research found. Example: "A ship's-mug shape for the Maritime Museum, glazed with clay from the slipway the museum stands on."

**Drafting rule (NON-NEGOTIABLE):** lead with the research, not product specs. The first message asks for a conversation. No pricing, minimums or PDFs. Present the concept as a rough starting idea, not a finished piece.
