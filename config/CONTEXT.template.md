## Follow-up Cadence

Business days. Maximum 2 follow-ups per target, then inactive.

| Deal type | First follow-up due | Second follow-up due | After 2 unanswered |
|---|---|---|---|
| PR | 5 after send | 5 after follow-up 1 | inactive |
| Corporate Gifting | 5 after send | 5 after follow-up 1 | inactive |
| Wholesale | 3 after send | 4 after follow-up 1 | inactive, revisit next quarter |
| Exclusive Partnership | 7 after send | 7 after follow-up 1 | inactive, revisit next quarter |

Tone: shorter than the pitch, warmer, one line that re-contextualizes. Never repeat the full pitch.
